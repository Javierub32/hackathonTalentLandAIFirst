### Q1 · Existing AI job coaches and their gaps

- Generic AI interview simulators abound: Toptrabajos (Spanish web, video answers scored 0-100 with STAR analysis, first session free), MeeTalent, Evalio, Elevate Interview, AIApply, JobWhiz, Huru, Final Round AI. [Toptrabajos](https://www.toptrabajos.com/simulador-de-entrevista) · [AIApply](https://aiapply.co/es/ai-job-interview) · [MeeTalent](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/asistente-ai-meetalent/id6742232505) · [Evalio](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/evalio-ai-interview-cv/id6748534114?l=es-MX). So what: "AI mock interview" alone is a commodity; it cannot be our VENTAJA COMPETITIVA (R16).
- They are B2C, aimed at digitally fluent candidates (often tech or office roles), paid by the candidate, with no human professional in the loop and no adaptation to profile (age, no experience, low digital skills). Inferred from the listings above (no source says otherwise). So what: the gap is profile-specific coaching + a human counsellor + an institution that pays.
- The public sector is starting to move: SEPE has an AI job-search assistant (press). [Diario de León](https://www.diariodeleon.es/leon/250717/2054401/sepe-revoluciona-busqueda-empleo-inteligencia-artificial-te-ayuda-encontrar-trabajo.html). Labora (Valencia regional employment service) runs a pilot of AI interview training. [Valencia Plaza](https://valenciaplaza.com/valenciaplaza/plaza-innovacion/labora-impulsa-un-proyecto-piloto-de-entrenamiento-de-entrevistas-de-trabajo-con-ia). So what: a public employment service already pays for this in another region: proof the customer exists. Name Labora as precedent and as a competitor in the pitch; check what SEPE's tool really does before the pitch.
- Senior-specific initiatives: Talento 45+ (Cámara de Comercio, ESF-funded orientation for ages 45-60) [SEPE](https://sepe.es/noticia/SEPE/2026/Junio/poder-reinventarse-iniciativa-empleabilidad-mayores-45-anios); Senia (marketplace for 50+ talent, Mapfre award, AI planned only for skills matching) [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=130206); Diagui (Argentina, AI assistant for 45+ re-entry) [Memo](https://www.memo.com.ar/economia/lanzan-diagui-la-inteligencia-artificial-que-guia-a-la-generacion-silver/). So what: these are human programmes or matching, not interview/ATS coaching. The closest analogue (Diagui) is outside Spain. Two named alternatives for R16.1.
- Fundación Generation's CEO says 45+ are the largest group of unemployed and that one barrier is lower exposure to digital tools such as AI. [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/economia/empleo/estela-de-la-cruz-fundacion-generation-la-busqueda-de-empleo-no-se-toma-vacaciones.html). So what: supports R14 (accessible UX: voice, plain Spanish) and suggests the coach should also teach how AI screening works.

### Q2 · How ATS and AI interviews filter, and their bias

- Adoption figures in Spain vary from 5% to 75% depending on what is measured: 5% of firms use AI in selection (InfoJobs/SPEC, via Forbes) [Forbes](https://forbes.es/?p=324521); 42% value automated CV screening (ManpowerGroup Q3 2026, via Que.es) [Que.es](https://www.que.es/2026/08/14/como-hacer-cv-para-ia/); "75% of applications are not reviewed by people" (study cited by El Español, scope unclear) [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/sociedad/20260319/ia-cambia-mercado-laboral-solicitudes-empleo-no-revisan-personas-estudio/1003744173507_0.html). So what: in the pitch, cite the conservative, best-sourced figure (ManpowerGroup 42%) and do not oversell; it is a growing trend, not yet universal.
- 90% of unemployed people over 50 believe their CV does not pass the filter because of their age (Fundación Adecco 2024, 600 respondents); 75% report ageism in selection processes (2025). [Fundación Adecco 2025](https://fundacionadecco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/170625NDP-TUEDADESUNTESORO-.pdf) · [2024](https://fundacionadecco.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/190624NDP-TUEDADESUNTESORO-.pdf). So what: the felt pain is "the filter discards me before a human sees me". The ATS angle is real for the senior persona.
- 73% of entry-level candidates suspect AI blocked their applications (Intuition Labs, international, via a secondary source). [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=94644). 51.7% of Spanish firms expect fewer junior openings because of AI. [OKdiario Talent24h](https://talent24h.okdiario.com/?p=24351). So what: the young persona has the same "invisible filter" pain, and more competition per junior job. Weak source: confirm with our own survey (R15).
- HireVue was criticised for facial analysis (EPIC complaint to the FTC) and later dropped facial analysis after an audit, keeping language analysis. [Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/carreira/2019/11/por-que-a-inteligencia-artificial-pode-ser-uma-ameaca-para-igualdade-no-trabalho/) · [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9550152). So what: AI interviews judge what you say and how you structure it. That can be trained, and that is our coaching target. No direct study on age bias was found.
- EU AI Act: AI for recruitment and screening of applications is **high-risk** (Annex III, point 4), and emotion recognition in the workplace is **prohibited** (Art. 5(1)(f)). [AI Act Service Desk, Annex III](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/es/ai-act/annex-3) · [artificialintelligenceact.eu](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/es/annex/3/). So what: our coach is on the candidate's side and makes no hiring decision, so it is not high-risk. We must not analyse emotions or faces (R13), and the pitch can use the regulation as a reason why candidates need to understand these systems.

### Q3 · Data to size the problem and the persona

- Spain: just over 1 million people aged 45+ are unemployed (EPA), about 55% of them women; 45+ account for about three quarters of long-term unemployment. [SEPE Observatorio, 45+ report 2026](https://www.sepe.es/HomeSepe/que-es-observatorio/publicaciones-observatorio/detalle-publicacion-observatorio~_publicaciones_publicaciones-observatorio_RESUMENES-EJECUTIVOS-2026_imt-2026-mayoresde45~.html). So what: the headline figure for R1.2 and R9.2.
- Long-term unemployment is 36.4% of all unemployment (EPA Q4 2025). [MITES EPA 2025 T4](https://www.mites.gob.es/ficheros/ministerio/sec_trabajo/analisis_mercado_trabajo/EPA/2025/Cuarto_trimestre/EPA-2025-T4-MITES-SGEAS.pdf). For people over 50, 55.4% have been looking for more than a year, 545,143 receive the unemployment subsidy (April 2025), and 65% of long-term senior unemployed say they lost self-esteem. [Fundación Adecco 2025](https://fundacionadecco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/170625NDP-TUEDADESUNTESORO-.pdf). So what: the problem is both practical and emotional; the coach should rebuild confidence as well as teach (Human + AI, R2.2).
- Andalucía: 305,742 of 536,523 unemployed (57%) are 45+ (June 2026). [Andalucía Informa / elDiario](https://andaluciainforma.eldiario.es/?p=34568). So what: a local, sized figure for a Málaga/Andalucía persona (R9.2).
- Young people: youth unemployment (under 25) in Andalucía is 31.7% (EPA, end of 2025) [Esdiario](https://www.esdiario.com/andalucia/260127/178099/andalucia-bate-record-ocupados-baja-paro-30-000-personas.html); in Málaga, unemployment among under-30s is 22.5% in 2025, a record low (Observatorio Argos, SAE) [Que.es](https://www.que.es/2026/05/25/paro-joven-malaga-minimo-historico/); Málaga's general rate is 13.5% (Q2 2026) [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/malaga/economia/20260428/tasa-paro-sube-malaga-inicio-destruyen-empleos/1003744225589_0.html). Monthly youth tables: [INJUVE](https://injuve.es/sites/default/files/adjuntos/2025/06/ParoReg2025-05_16a24_Tablas_0.pdf), [MITES Informe Jóvenes](https://www.mites.gob.es/ficheros/ministerio/sec_trabajo/analisis_mercado_trabajo/jovenes/2025/Informe_Jovenes_Num47_Noviembre_2025.pdf). So what: the youth problem is real but improving in Málaga. The senior problem is larger and more neglected, which points to senior long-term unemployed as the main persona and young people as a second segment.

Promálaga focuses on entrepreneurs, not unemployed people. [Fundación Unicaja](https://www.fundacionunicaja.com/blog/el-talento-del-futuro-se-da-cita-en-talent-land-espana-2026-con-el-patrocinio-de-fundacion-unicaja/). So what: viable as a paying customer **only for the young-graduate segment**, and familiar to this jury (the organisers used "Universidad de Málaga" as their example of a concrete customer). But UMA graduates are a weak fit for R1 (vulnerable group): 75% are employed within four years, and the service exists already. - Existing job boards for this segment: SEPE's **Empléate** already lists 29,000+ offers for 45+ with about 84,000 companies posting [EPALE](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/es/resource-centre/content/empleate); Krisalidas (senior talent platform, Oct 2025) [The Objective](https://theobjective.com/economia/empleo/2025-10-27/krisalidas-plataforma-impulsa-talento-senior/); Senia (50+ marketplace) [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=130206); Alianza +45 (Madrid city council's employment agency, Fundación Máshumano, Turium) [Emprendedores](https://emprendedores.es/ayudas/alianza-45-nace-para-promover-el-valor-de-la-experiencia/); Comunidad de Madrid Plan de Empleo Sénior 2026-2027 [Comunidad de Madrid](https://www.comunidad.madrid/servicios/empleo/plan-empleo-senior). ## So what for Brainstorm

1. A generic AI mock interview is a commodity (Toptrabajos, AIApply and others). The space nobody covers is profile-specific coaching (45+ long-term unemployed, first-job youth) on ATS and AI interviews, with a human counsellor in the loop.
2. Persona (team decision, 2026-11-24): 45+ long-term unemployed in Málaga (Andalucía: 57% of the unemployed are 45+; 55% of over-50s search for more than a year; 90% believe the filter rejects them for their age). Young people and UMA are dropped.
3. Paying customer candidates in Málaga: IMFE (Ayuntamiento de Málaga: T-Acompañamos, Aquileo+ with €10M of ESF+) or Andalucía Orienta / SAE (€2.7M for Málaga). B2G/B2B2C, not paid by the candidate. UMA was considered and dropped (young graduates only, weak fit for R1).
4. End-to-end companion (coach + CV/ATS + interviews + offers): employers have a reason to post (78% struggle to fill vacancies) and a 45+ hook (Emplea-T, up to €18,000 per permanent hire). A job board alone is not new (Empléate, Krisalidas, Senia), so the coach is the core and the offers are the closing step; seed them with public offers to avoid an empty marketplace. Precedent: Labora (Valencia) already pilots AI interview training. Use it as proof that the customer exists and differentiate on profile adaptation and the ATS focus.
5. Pain is emotional as well as practical (65% lost self-esteem), so the coach should rebuild confidence and offer accessible UX (voice, plain Spanish) for low digital skills.
6. AI Act: emotion recognition at work is prohibited and recruiter-side screening is high-risk. Stay on the candidate's side, make no hiring decisions and analyse no faces or emotions.
7. Validation today: survey 45+ and young unemployed people; ask mentors (Diverxia) for an introduction to IMFE, the SAE or an Andalucía Orienta entity (R15.2). Free AI: Gemini or Groq free tier.
